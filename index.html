Questions

Before you start.

Do I pay to use the website designer?

No. You can create and customize the design first. The paid package is for JowkariTech to finish and launch the production website.

What if I already own my domain?

Keep it. We connect the finished website to your existing domain. We do not need your domain password.

Can you add an AI chatbot to the site?

Yes. The Pro package can include an AI website assistant configured around your business information and lead-capture flow.

Can you build something more custom than a normal website?

Yes. JowkariTech can also build web apps, customer portals, booking flows, payment features and business automations when the project needs them.
