Questions

What business owners usually want to know.

Do I have to pay to build the website?

No. You can generate and customize the website before paying. Launch is a separate step.

Is this only for North Vancouver?

No. JowkariTech is based in North Vancouver and focuses heavily on Metro Vancouver, but the builder can be used by businesses across Canada.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. Keep your existing domain. Domain connection is handled during launch, and you should never send us your domain password.

Can I change the generated website?

Yes. That is the point of the builder. Change text, pages, sections, colours, fonts, photos, logo and layout before requesting launch.

Does the site automatically guarantee more customers?

No website can honestly guarantee customers. The product is designed to make it easier for visitors to understand your services, call, request a quote or book the next step.
