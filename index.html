A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear from
the start.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. Studio is free to create, edit and export a website. A temporary shared preview lasts 30 minutes, with a three-hour cooldown before creating a new one. You can update an active preview.

What is included in the C$150 build?

A focused website design and production setup. We confirm the pages, content, domain connection and any extra requirements before starting. Domain renewal and paid third-party services are not included forever.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. You keep ownership of your domain. We can help with the connection during launch; you should never send passwords through an enquiry form.

Will my website automatically send enquiries?

The free exported site opens an email draft. A managed production launch can include a connected contact form; the destination and delivery are tested as part of that scope.

Does this guarantee more customers?

No. A clearer website can make it easier to understand and contact your business. Traffic, reputation, pricing and other factors still matter; there is no guaranteed ranking or customer count.
