Questions

Simple answers.

Is the website designer actually free?

Yes. You can create and customize the design without paying. The free preview link lasts 30 minutes. You pay only if you want JowkariTech to turn that design into the production website.

Can I send the preview link to someone else?

Yes. The builder generates a unique preview link for your design. Anyone with that link can view it during the 30-minute preview window.

What if many businesses choose the same type?

Each design has its own information, layout choices and unique preview link, so multiple roofers, plumbers or other businesses can use the same starting category without overwriting each other.

Do I need to buy another domain?

No. If you already own a domain, keep it. We connect the finished website to that domain. You do not need to send us your domain password.

Do I have to buy the AI chatbot?

No. The website and chatbot are separate. You can launch the website without AI and add the chatbot later.
