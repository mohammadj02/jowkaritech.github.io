SUPPORT TERMS · UPDATED 2026-10-15

Clear before
we connect.

No paid work before an agreed scope, price and appointment. No hidden remote access.

A request is not an appointment.

Submitting a form is free. We first review whether remote help is suitable. A session begins only after the scope, total price in CAD, date, time zone and payment terms are agreed. No subscription is activated by the request.

Scope and pricing.

Basic Remote Support is C$45 CAD for up to 30 minutes on one straightforward issue. Extended Remote Support is C$80 CAD for up to 90 minutes on one agreed troubleshooting issue. Official recovery assistance starts at C$45 CAD; business IT, Linux and infrastructure work is quoted separately. Any applicable tax and paid extras are disclosed in the agreed total before work begins.

The appointment can include diagnosis, attempted troubleshooting and an explanation of the outcome. We do not guarantee a fix, data recovery or account access. We stop before exceeding the agreed scope or time unless you authorize additional work at an agreed price.

Your control during the session.

You must own the device or have the owner's and organization's authorization. Stay present during remote access. You can refuse a change or end the connection. Keep a suitable backup and tell us about any critical business requirements before work.

We never bypass passwords, encryption, two-step verification or account-provider controls. Enter private login information yourself on official pages with sharing stopped. No unattended access or session recording is authorized by the intake checkbox.

Cancellation, rescheduling and payment.

Cancel or reschedule by email before the agreed appointment start with no cancellation charge. If JowkariTech cannot attend, we arrange another time or cancel without charging for unperformed work. Once paid troubleshooting has started, the written session terms agreed beforehand determine the amount due.

We do not collect payment-card details in the support form, remote chat or ordinary email. Payment uses the method confirmed in the agreed terms. No payment processor or automatic checkout is activated on this page. Contact us promptly about any billing or service concern.

Work that needs another provider.

Physical repairs, devices without a viable connection, specialist data recovery, employer-managed restrictions and account-provider decisions may require a different service. This is appointment-based general IT help, not a 24/7 incident-response or emergency service. These terms do not remove rights you may have under applicable law.
