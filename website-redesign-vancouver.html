When a redesign makes sense

The problem is usually clarity, trust or friction—not the age of the colour palette.

An old website may still contain useful service information and search history. The redesign process should preserve what helps while fixing the parts that make the business harder to trust or contact.

01

Hard to use on mobile

Tiny text, buried calls-to-action and desktop-first layouts cost attention before the visitor understands the offer.

02

Outdated or unclear offer

Old services, stale pricing, inconsistent contact information and generic copy make the business feel less current than it really is.

03

Weak search structure

Missing titles, thin pages, broken internal links and unclear service hierarchy make it harder for search engines to understand the site.

FAQ

Website redesign FAQ

Can I keep my existing domain?

Yes. A redesign normally keeps the existing domain unless there is a separate business reason to change it.

What happens to old URLs?

Important old URLs should be retained or redirected to the closest relevant new page so users and search engines do not land on errors.

Can you redesign a site from Wix, Squarespace or WordPress?

JowkariTech can rebuild the public-facing website independently of the old platform. Exact migration needs depend on the existing content, forms, ecommerce, booking and integrations.

Will a redesign improve rankings?

It can improve technical quality and relevance, but ranking outcomes are not guaranteed. A poor migration can also hurt visibility, which is why URL mapping and indexing checks matter.

Can I see the new direction before launch?

Yes. The visual builder is useful for testing the new design direction before production launch.
