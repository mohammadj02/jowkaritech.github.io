Technical help with an agreed boundary.

Small-business problems are not always a full IT department’s worth of work. JowkariTech offers scoped remote troubleshooting for Windows, Linux and website infrastructure. Send the symptom and business impact, not credentials or customer records.

The founder has hands-on experience with Linux, Windows, networking, Python, Docker, Caddy, Cloudflare and web applications. We confirm whether the particular stack and problem fit that experience before accepting a project.

Typical projects.

  • Outlook, Office and small-team workstation configuration.
  • Linux configuration and service troubleshooting, with a backup and rollback plan.
  • Domain and DNS issues, mail records and website routing.
  • HTTPS and certificate configuration, Caddy and Cloudflare troubleshooting.
  • Docker application configuration and deployment diagnosis.
  • Website issues alongside our existing web design and AI services.

Before changing a production system.

We identify the owner, your authority, the affected service and what must keep working. Agree on a maintenance window, backup, access method and rollback plan. Credentials are entered through the appropriate provider tools, not collected in this intake.

Destructive operations, account-owner changes and expanded scope require separate approval. We do not offer a 24/7 monitoring contract, guaranteed incident response or a security-compliance certification through this service.

Business support is quoted in CAD.

Business IT, Linux, DNS and website work uses a custom written quote. A simple workstation issue may fit the C$45 or C$80 remote-support options after review. We do not silently convert an enquiry into an ongoing subscription.

  1. Tell us what is happening. Submit the symptom, device and preferred time. No passwords or codes.
  2. We check remote suitability. Hardware or account-provider restrictions may need a different route.
  3. Agree on the price and appointment. Your time zone, scope and payment terms are confirmed before work.
  4. You approve an attended session. Stay present, control what is shared, and end access whenever you choose.
  5. We troubleshoot and explain. Get a plain-language outcome and next steps, including any limitations.
  6. Pay under the agreed terms. No subscription or extra work without a separate agreement.

Business support questions.

Can you work on an employer-managed laptop?

Only with the organization’s authorization and within its policies. Existing IT staff should approve any remote tool or administrative change.

Can you promise no downtime?

No. We discuss the risk and a maintenance window before changes. Backups, verification and rollback are part of the agreed technical plan.

Can you access confidential customer files?

We ask you to close unrelated records and use the minimum access needed. Do not paste confidential data into the request. Any special privacy requirements must be agreed before work.
