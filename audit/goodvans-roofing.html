3 conversion improvements for GoodVans Roofing

This is a short independent audit prepared for GoodVans Roofing. It is not a redesign proposal yet—just three specific opportunities I noticed while reviewing the public website.

1Fix the visible “0” proof counters

On the public About page as indexed, the proof section currently shows 0 Roofs Installed, 0 Years Combined Experience and other zero-value counters. Even if this is only a front-end loading issue, it can weaken trust at the exact point where a homeowner is deciding whether to request a quote.

2Make the quote path feel shorter

The site has multiple contact and quote entry points. I would simplify the mobile path to one persistent “Get a roofing estimate” action and a short first-step form, then collect the extra project details after the visitor commits.

3Turn your team and project history into stronger proof

GoodVans already shows a real team and broad service coverage. I would move the strongest trust elements—team, certifications, recent project photos and review proof—closer to the first quote CTA so visitors see credibility before they are asked to fill out a form.
