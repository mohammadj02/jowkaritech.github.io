3 conversion improvements for Heyes Roofing

Heyes already has something new contractors cannot manufacture: decades of North Shore history. The biggest opportunity is making that credibility work harder on mobile.

1Put the 30+ year North Shore story above the fold

Your public site says Heyes has served North and West Vancouver for more than 30 years and has operated from the same location since 1995. I would make that the first trust message visitors see beside the quote CTA instead of making them discover it deeper in the site.

2Reduce friction between “I need a roofer” and “request estimate”

I would give mobile visitors one obvious path: choose repair / replacement / inspection, enter location and contact details, and request a callback. That is easier than making a homeowner navigate several pages before taking action.

3Modernize presentation without losing the family-business feel

The goal would not be a flashy tech site. It would be a cleaner layout built around local history, project photos, warranties, service areas and a stronger estimate flow—keeping the established-business identity intact.
