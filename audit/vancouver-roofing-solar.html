3 conversion improvements for Vancouver Roofing & Solar

Your site already covers both roofing and solar. I would focus on making the first visit more local, more provable and easier to turn into a quote request.

1Separate roofing and solar intent immediately

A homeowner looking for a leak repair has a very different urgency from someone researching solar. I would split the first CTA into “Roofing estimate” and “Solar assessment” so each visitor gets a shorter, more relevant path.

2Strengthen proof around the testimonials

The homepage includes customer quotes. I would pair the strongest ones with real project photos, neighbourhood/location context and links to third-party review profiles where possible. That makes the proof easier for a new visitor to trust.

3Add a guided quote intake

A lightweight estimator can ask roof type, service needed, location and urgency before collecting contact details. It gives the visitor value immediately and gives your team a better-qualified inquiry.
