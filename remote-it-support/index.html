From your first message to the outcome.

  1. Tell us what is happening. Submit the symptom, device and preferred time. No passwords or codes.
  2. We check remote suitability. Hardware or account-provider restrictions may need a different route.
  3. Agree on the price and appointment. Your time zone, scope and payment terms are confirmed before work.
  4. You approve an attended session. Stay present, control what is shared, and end access whenever you choose.
  5. We troubleshoot and explain. Get a plain-language outcome and next steps, including any limitations.
  6. Pay under the agreed terms. No subscription or extra work without a separate agreement.

When remote computer repair is not the right fit.

The computer normally needs to start, connect to the internet and run an agreed screen-sharing tool. A damaged drive, a machine that will not power on, physical printer faults or a failed internet connection may require local hands-on help. We do not advertise on-site visits or a repair storefront.

Liquid damage, a burning smell, swollen battery, clicking drive or important files disappearing? Stop using the affected device and seek appropriate hands-on help. Do not start a remote clean-up or reinstall that could make recovery harder.

If the issue belongs to your employer or an account provider, get their authorization first. JowkariTech is an independent service, not a replacement for your provider’s identity checks.

Before you book.

Can I get help from elsewhere in Canada?

Yes. Remote appointments are offered across Canada, subject to suitability and availability. Include your time zone; a submitted preference is not a confirmed appointment.

Can you guarantee the issue will be fixed?

No. Paid time can include diagnosis and explaining next steps. Hardware, software defects and provider decisions may limit what can be resolved. We discuss scope before the session.

Will you ask for my password?

No. Enter passwords, verification codes and recovery information yourself on the official provider page while screen sharing and recording are stopped.

Do I need to install something now?

No. Wait until the appointment and the technician identity are confirmed. Agree on the remote tool then; never install remote software because an unexpected caller or popup tells you to.
