A direct conversation about your website.

I’m Mohammad, the founder of JowkariTech. I build websites and practical AI tools from North Vancouver, British Columbia. The starting point is straightforward: understand what your business does, what a visitor needs to know and how they should contact you.

Some owners want to explore the design themselves. Others would rather supply the facts and have the build handled for them. JowkariTech offers both an editable Website Studio and a focused done-for-you option.

What you can expect from the process.

  1. A factual brief. We use your services, service area, contact details and approved content. Photos, credentials and customer feedback must belong to your real business.
  2. An editable direction. A draft makes the conversation specific. You can inspect the layout and identify what is missing before discussing a production launch.
  3. A defined scope. Pages, integrations, domain connection and any ongoing costs are agreed before work begins. A small website and a custom web application are not the same project.
  4. A checked launch. The enquiry destination, page links and mobile experience need testing. A button that looks finished is not enough.

What the examples do—and do not—prove.

The websites in our concept portfolio are demonstrations of design approaches. They are not customer endorsements or claims that those fictional businesses hired JowkariTech. We do not present invented reviews, awards, years in business or customer numbers as evidence.

AI-generated writing is also a draft. It should not invent availability, professional credentials, warranties or prices. Business owners review the facts before publishing.

Keep control of the essentials.

Keep the domain registered to the business, and use secure provider access rather than emailing passwords. In Studio, save a downloadable project backup as well as the draft stored in your browser. A static website export can be hosted elsewhere; it does not include an automatically sending enquiry backend.

Use our website launch checklist to review those details. No design, SEO adjustment or AI feature comes with a guaranteed search position or customer count.
