FAQ

Affordable web design questions

Is the C$150 price monthly?

No. The C$150 done-for-you option is a flat website build/setup offer. Domain registration or renewal, paid third-party services and optional ongoing work can be separate.

Is a domain free forever?

No domain is truly free forever. If a domain is included or purchased for a project, renewal still occurs on the registrar's renewal cycle unless JowkariTech explicitly agrees to cover it.

Can I launch without a monthly plan?

Yes. The done-for-you flat option is intended for businesses that do not want a monthly design fee. Hosting/domain arrangements are confirmed based on the chosen setup.

Why is the builder free?

The builder lets you see the direction first. If you want JowkariTech to turn it into a production site, you can request launch afterward.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Additional pages, AI chatbot features, booking flows, automation and ongoing SEO work can be added later if the business needs them.
