Contractor website design FAQ

Which trades can use the builder?

The builder includes roofing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, heat pumps, renovation, landscaping, painting, cleaning, concrete, flooring, windows, doors and many other home-service categories.

Can I add project photos?

Yes. The editor lets you upload your own images or use image URLs for the design. Real project photos are strongly preferred for production sites.

Can the site collect quote requests?

Yes. Quote/contact forms are part of the launch approach. Form routing and final production behaviour are confirmed during launch.

Can you make service-area pages?

Yes, when the business genuinely serves those areas and each page contains useful local information rather than copy-paste text.

Do you guarantee leads?

No. A website can improve credibility and reduce friction, but lead volume depends on traffic, competition, reviews, reputation, pricing and many other factors.
