Outlook Not Receiving Emails? Check This First

Separate an Outlook application problem from an account or connection issue before changing settings.

JowkariTech support notes · Updated 2026-10-15 · General guidance; a specific diagnosis may need an appointment.

Identify which Outlook you useCompare the app with webmailCheck connection and storageProtect unsynced mailWhat to include in an enquiry

Identify which Outlook you use

New Outlook, classic Outlook and browser-based Outlook do not have identical controls. Also identify the email provider: an Outlook application may contain a Microsoft, work, school or another provider’s mailbox. Write down the symptom without copying message contents.

Compare the app with webmail

Check that ordinary websites load, then open your provider’s official webmail yourself. If webmail receives a test message but the app does not, focus on the app or its connection. If both fail, the account, storage or provider may need attention. Avoid repeatedly removing and adding the account while you are still identifying the cause.

Check connection and storage

Microsoft notes that full cloud storage can prevent sending and receiving mail. Review the provider’s storage information before deleting anything. In classic Outlook, inspect whether the status says Working Offline or Disconnected; the Work Offline toggle is under Send/Receive. Do not assume the same menu applies to new Outlook. A recent password change may also require signing in again through the official prompt.

Protect unsynced mail

Do not delete PST files, recreate profiles or clear caches just because a forum suggests it. Locally stored or unsynced items need to be understood and backed up first. For an employer-managed mailbox, contact its administrator before changing the account or security settings.

What to include in an enquiry

Say which Outlook version and provider you use, whether webmail works, and the approximate start time. Mention any storage warning or error wording after removing private information. Our support form does not need message subjects, mailbox exports, passwords or verification codes.
