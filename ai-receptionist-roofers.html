Why an AI receptionist can fit a roofing business

Potential customers often visit a website because they have a specific question or want to know whether a company can help. If no one is available to reply immediately, many visitors simply leave.

A JowkariTech AI receptionist gives those visitors a simple conversation. It can be configured around your approved business information and focus on common roofing topics such as repairs, replacements, inspections, leaks, and estimate requests.

What it can collect

  • Customer name and contact details
  • The type of service they need
  • Location or service-area information
  • Preferred day or time for follow-up
  • Important details you normally ask before providing a quote

What it does not replace

It does not replace professional judgment, site visits, or your team. It is designed to make your website better at starting conversations and collecting inquiries.
