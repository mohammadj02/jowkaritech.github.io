Use the chatbot for jobs it can actually do well.

The best small-business chatbot has a narrow, useful role: answer approved business questions, collect customer details, and make the handoff to a person clear.

01

Common questions

Small-business AI chatbot FAQ

Can the chatbot answer customers 24/7?

The software can be available continuously, but its answers should stay within the approved information and clear limitations configured for the business.

Can it collect leads?

Yes. It can collect contact information and inquiry details for follow-up, subject to the site's privacy practices and the information you choose to request.

Will it replace my staff?

That is not the goal. For most small businesses, the useful role is handling repetitive questions and starting the lead-intake process before a person takes over.
