AI website builder FAQ

Is the website builder really using AI?

Yes. Jowkari Sites calls JowkariTech's Cloudflare Workers AI endpoint to draft the initial website copy. The existing deterministic template remains as a fallback if AI generation fails.

Does AI publish the website automatically?

No. AI generates the starting copy and the visual editor lets you customize it. Publishing to a production domain is a separate launch step.

Will it invent reviews or awards?

The generation prompt explicitly prohibits invented reviews, awards, licenses, guarantees, years in business and customer counts. You should still verify every business fact before publishing.

Is it free?

Generating and editing the starting website is free to try. Production hosting, custom-domain launch or done-for-you work can involve paid plans.

Can I use it outside Vancouver?

Yes. The product is being marketed locally in Metro Vancouver, but businesses across Canada can use the builder.
