FAQ

AI website builder FAQ

Is the website builder really using AI?

The Details panel offers Refine this page with AI through JowkariTech's Cloudflare AI service. Initial creation uses the local starter layout. AI is optional; editing and static export do not require a successful AI call.

Does AI publish the website automatically?

No. You choose when to use AI writing and review the draft yourself. A shared preview is temporary. Exporting a static website or arranging a production launch is a separate action.

Will it invent reviews or awards?

The generation prompt explicitly prohibits invented reviews, awards, licenses, guarantees, years in business and customer counts. You should still verify every business fact before publishing.

Is it free?

Creating, editing and exporting a static website is free. A managed production launch, a custom domain or third-party integrations can involve separate costs.

Can I use it outside Vancouver?

Yes. The product is being marketed locally in Metro Vancouver, but businesses across Canada can use the builder.
