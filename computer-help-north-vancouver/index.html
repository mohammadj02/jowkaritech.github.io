How a remote appointment works.

  1. Tell us what is happening. Submit the symptom, device and preferred time. No passwords or codes.
  2. We check remote suitability. Hardware or account-provider restrictions may need a different route.
  3. Agree on the price and appointment. Your time zone, scope and payment terms are confirmed before work.
  4. You approve an attended session. Stay present, control what is shared, and end access whenever you choose.
  5. We troubleshoot and explain. Get a plain-language outcome and next steps, including any limitations.
  6. Pay under the agreed terms. No subscription or extra work without a separate agreement.

Questions from local customers.

Can I bring my laptop to you?

This website does not offer walk-in repairs or a storefront. Use the request form so we can assess remote suitability. Physical repairs may need a local repair specialist.

Do you cover Lynn Valley, Lonsdale and Deep Cove?

The remote service is available to customers in North Vancouver, including those areas, when a working internet connection and a suitable device are available. This is not a claim of offices in those neighbourhoods.

What time zone should I select?

Choose America/Vancouver for a North Vancouver appointment. We confirm the actual date and time by email before a session begins.
