Your information.
Only what is needed.

A practical explanation of the remote-support request process and its limits.

What we ask for and why.

The support form collects your name, reply email, optional phone number, device and operating-system category, problem description, preferred appointment time, time zone and consent. We use these to assess suitability, reply, schedule agreed support and deliver the service. The form does not collect payment-card information or grant remote access.

Never include passwords, tokens, security answers, account-recovery codes, verification codes, identity documents or confidential customer records. There is no attachment upload on the support form.

Where a support request goes.

The website uses GitHub Pages. Requests pass through a Cloudflare Worker and the existing Web3Forms email service to JowkariTech's business inbox. These providers may process information outside Canada under their own policies. We do not promise Canada-only storage.

The Worker does not write contact fields or problem descriptions into public website files, application logs or preview storage. It keeps a non-reversible request fingerprint, receipt state and timestamp for up to 24 hours to reduce duplicate notifications. Anti-spam checks use a keyed hash rather than storing your raw IP in that receipt. Hosting and email providers can still process ordinary connection and delivery metadata.

Consent, retention and deletion.

The checkbox authorizes a reply about this request, not marketing. A separate agreement and explicit attended-session approval are needed before screen sharing. Do not share unrelated private windows. Sessions are not recorded by JowkariTech unless separately agreed in writing.

Enquiry emails remain in the business inbox until deleted under the operational retention process. Unneeded declined or abandoned enquiries are reviewed for deletion after 90 days; this is an owner-managed review, not an automatic mailbox purge. Records needed for an active service, payment dispute or required business recordkeeping may need to be kept longer.

To ask about access, correction or deletion, contact jowkaritech@gmail.com. Tell us enough to locate the enquiry, but do not email sensitive identity documents unsolicited.

Measurement without support details.

The new support layer does not install an advertising or analytics SDK. It exposes conversion hooks containing only an approved event name, a public page path and an approved source channel. Names, emails, device details and descriptions are not included.

Approved campaign labels and a public source-page path may be held in session storage and reused for up to 30 minutes to attribute an enquiry. No customer description is stored there. The existing website builder stores its own website drafts locally; it is a separate product from the support form.
