## 7. Seven-day launch checklist

### Day 1 — make the first appointment possible
Check the deployed support page and submit one clearly marked test. Confirm it reaches the business inbox; archive it as QA, not a lead. Set realistic appointment windows around your actual availability. Choose an attended remote-support tool whose current commercial terms support your use and test it on a consenting spare device. Do not start unsolicited remote sessions. Confirm your written price and payment method before charging anyone.

### Day 2 — warm referrals
Share the referral message with a small number of relevant contacts who welcome it. Ask them to forward the page rather than sending you somebody else's private support details. Count real responses, not views.

### Day 3 — one service listing
Publish the Kijiji listing in a permitted service category. Use the actual C$45 starting/basic price and the live page. Keep the source-labelled link. Do not purchase promotion automatically.

### Day 4 — one local community introduction
Ask the group administrator or check Nextdoor's business-posting route. Publish one appropriate introduction. Reply to questions without collecting private data in comments or pretending to be a customer.

### Day 5 — follow up on requested help
Reply to people who have asked for assistance. Establish suitability, scope, time zone, appointment time and total price. A support form is a lead, not a booked appointment or payment.

### Day 6 — deliver and document
For a consenting customer, confirm their identity/authority and the agreed session. Keep access attended; stop sharing during credential entry. Record the non-sensitive outcome and amount paid privately. Ask for honest feedback after real service; never write your own customer reviews.

### Day 7 — review what produced an actual customer
Count requests, suitable requests, appointments, completed sessions, paying customers and collected CAD revenue by source. Compare listings based on this funnel, not a tiny search-position average. Improve one weak step. SEO is a medium-term channel, not a promise of a customer tomorrow.
