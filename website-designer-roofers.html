Plan a roofing website around the estimate brief.

A repair enquiry and a replacement enquiry are different starting points. Explain the roofing work you actually offer and give visitors a way to describe the property, service area and issue. Only advertise emergency availability, warranties or certifications that your business can support.

For real project photos, a useful caption identifies the kind of work and what changed without exposing a customer’s private details. A close-up needs context; a finished roof image should not be presented as your project when it is stock photography.

For the initial estimate request, consider contact details, the project type and an optional short description. A production photo-upload field needs its own storage, permissions and file handling; it is not automatically provided by a static export. Test one submission to the real destination before promoting the site.
