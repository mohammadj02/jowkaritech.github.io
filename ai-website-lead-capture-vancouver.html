Built for businesses that cannot answer every website visitor immediately

Roofers, plumbers, HVAC companies, cleaners, electricians, landscapers and similar businesses spend much of the day doing the actual work. A potential customer may visit the website while nobody is available to answer a question or collect the details needed for a quote.

JowkariTech adds a conversational layer to the existing website. The assistant can answer business-approved FAQs and ask the visitor for the information your team normally needs before following up.

Information it can collect

  • Name, phone and email
  • Requested service
  • Property or job location
  • Urgency and timing
  • Job details relevant to the trade
  • Preferred callback window

What it should not do

It should not invent project pricing, diagnose unsafe situations, guarantee availability, or replace professional judgment. The assistant is configured around information approved by the business.
