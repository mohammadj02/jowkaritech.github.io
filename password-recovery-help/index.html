Help with the process. Not a way around it.

A forgotten password, a Windows PIN problem and a locked account are different issues. We help you identify which one you are facing and find the official recovery route. The work is for the legitimate account owner or an authorized administrator only.

You enter all passwords, security answers, recovery codes and verification codes yourself on the provider’s official site, with sharing stopped. Do not send these details in the support form or by email.

What assistance can include.

  • Distinguishing a Windows device PIN from a Microsoft account password.
  • Finding the provider’s official sign-in helper or recovery form.
  • Understanding which non-secret account details you should gather yourself.
  • Troubleshooting the browser or device used for recovery.
  • Discussing safer sign-in and recovery settings after you regain access.

Where our help ends.

We cannot override identity checks, unlock somebody else’s account, defeat two-step verification, bypass encryption or guarantee access. Managed work and school accounts usually need their own IT administrator.

Microsoft documents restrictions when two-step verification is enabled and its recovery methods are unavailable. Even Microsoft support cannot simply change account details to bypass those checks. Read the official Microsoft recovery guidance before paying anyone who promises access.

From C$45 CAD. The quote covers agreed recovery assistance, not a successful recovery. We review suitability first and tell you when the provider, your administrator or another official route is the appropriate next step.

Your recovery questions.

Can you get into an account without its owner?

No. We require confirmation that you are the owner or an authorized administrator. We will stop if ownership or authority is unclear.

Should I send a screenshot of the recovery page?

Do not attach passwords, codes, recovery answers or identity documents. The intake has no file upload. Describe the general message without private details.

Can I try the official route for free first?

Yes. Provider recovery tools are available directly. Our paid service is optional assistance navigating the process, not special access to the provider.
